**Target Audience:** Sellers

### Subject Line Options:

- "The First 2 Weeks Can Make or Break Your Sale"

### Content Ideas:

1. **Pricing Psychology**
   - Why pricing at $499k vs $500k matters
   - Search filter cutoffs buyers use
   - Buyer perception of "just under" pricing

2. **Days on Market Impact**
   - The first 2 weeks are critical
   - Stale listings get lowballed
   - Price reductions signal desperation

3. **Pre-Listing Inspection Option**
   - Fix issues before buyers find them
   - Avoid last-minute negotiation leverage
   - Shows transparency and confidence

4. **Net Proceeds vs Sale Price**
   - Commissions eat into profit
   - Closing costs sellers pay
   - Repairs, concessions, credits add up
   - Create a realistic net sheet early

5. **Showings Flexibility**
   - Listings that are hard to show sell slower
   - Lockbox vs appointment-only impact
   - Weekend availability matters most
