What This Actually Means

When people ask, "What's the best neighborhood in Grand Rapids?" what they're really asking is one of these:

  • "Where can I get character and walkability?"
  • "Where are the best schools?"
  • "Where do I get the most house for my money?"

And each of those answers points to a different area.

So... What's the Best Suburb for You?

It depends on:

  • Price range
  • Lifestyle (walkable vs. suburban)
  • Schools
  • Commute
  • Stage of life

There's no universal "best." But there is a best fit.
