Alger Heights

Welcoming neighborhood in southeast Grand Rapids with affordable housing options, good schools, and a strong neighborhood association.

Schools Affordable Community

Belknap Lookout

Hilltop neighborhood with some of the best views of downtown Grand Rapids. Tight-knit community with active neighborhood association.

Views Hilltop Community

Creston / Riverside Gardens

Historic northeast Grand Rapids neighborhood with strong community identity. Features local businesses along Plainfield Avenue and Riverside Park.

Historic Parks Local Shops

Garfield Park

Named for the beautiful 18-acre park at its center, this diverse neighborhood offers affordable homes, community gardens, and a welcoming atmosphere.

Parks Affordable Gardens

John Ball Park

Welcoming neighborhood adjacent to John Ball Zoo and Park. Affordable homes, great parks access, and convenient west side location.

Zoo Affordable Parks

West Grand

Established neighborhood near John Ball Zoo with tree-lined streets and classic Grand Rapids architecture. Easy access to downtown and west side amenities.

Classic Convenient Tree-lined

Boston Square / Baxter

An up-and-coming neighborhood with increasing investment and community development. Affordable prices, diverse community, and easy downtown access.

Emerging Investment Diverse

Covell / Leonard Street

Northwest Grand Rapids neighborhood with affordable housing options and a suburban feel while still being close to downtown. Great value for buyers seeking more space.

Value Suburban Space

Eastown

Eclectic neighborhood known for its bohemian vibe, local shops, and diverse dining options along Wealthy Street. Strong arts community and historic homes.

Arts Dining Walkable

East Hills / Wealthy Street

A vibrant, walkable neighborhood featuring eclectic shops, restaurants, and cafes along Wealthy Street. Popular with professionals and creatives seeking urban living with character.

Walkable Urban Dining

Heritage Hill

Grand Rapids' premier historic district with stunning Victorian, Tudor, and Colonial Revival architecture. One of the largest urban historic districts in the country.

Historic Victorian Downtown

East Grand Rapids / Breton Village

Affluent city featuring top-rated schools, Reeds Lake, Gaslight Village shopping district, and beautifully maintained homes. Premier address in the metro area.

Top Schools Lakefront Walkable

Downtown Grand Rapids

The heart of West Michigan featuring world-class dining, entertainment, museums, and luxury condominiums. Urban living at its finest with walkable access to everything.

Urban Entertainment Condos
